# آخرین پناهگاه · بازبینی نمای جانبی

**Review status:** Gate 1 implemented and smoke-tested; Gate 2 samples ready for owner feedback. Gate 3 has not begun. This is not the finished art-and-combat slice.

## Open the playable review

Import `project.godot` in **Godot 4.7.2 Stable Standard**, let asset imports finish, and press **F5**. The game now boots into `scenes/side/Court.tscn` at a 960×540 logical resolution, with a strictly side-on, fixed-height camera. The earlier `Main.tscn`, `PersianRTL.tscn` and depth-lane `Depot.tscn` are unchanged legacy developer fixtures accessible by F6; there is no in-game link to them. Their historical checklists still apply to those scenes.

- **A/D** move; **Space** jumps; **Shift** sprints; **Ctrl** dodges on the floor without invulnerability. Try walking into the low case first, then jumping over it. W/S no longer move the character along a depth lane.
- **W** opens a contextual entrance only within reach and on the floor. Enter at the amber door, then press W again at the matching interior exit. Check that the player arrives at the linked marker and health, ammunition and Walker damage survive the round trip. A transfer freezes the outgoing room and fades to/from black; clearing the Walker doesn't lock the door.
- **Mouse/LMB** aims/fires; **R** reloads; **F** is obstruction-tested melee; **Esc** pauses; **Enter** restarts after defeat. Shot trajectories use the live mouse direction while airborne as well as on the floor. The review art shows a horizontal pistol only; angled weapon cels and final muzzle presentation are deliberately not implemented yet.
- **F2** toggles the moving rain; **F3** toggles the dynamic lamp's shadow pass (not the baked amber wash); **F11** toggles fullscreen. The window is resizable with integer scaling. A full settings UI, saved rebinding, camera zoom options, E interactions and Tab inventory are **not** part of this build. Action mappings can be changed in Godot's Input Map. The active HUD and prompts are Persian; the standard edition's RTL rendering still needs a graphical check.

## What to review at Gate 2

- [Native-size composition, 960×540](review/side_native.png). An offline layout using the **actual** in-game texture dimensions, not a Godot screenshot. The survivor's standing cel is 100 px tall; the Walker's is 104 px. Review the cyan bag and copper shell against the wall, the Walker's helmet and slumped posture, and how well both silhouettes hold up next to the warm doorway.
- [Short motion-key study](review/side_pose_study.gif). A survivor guard, contact stride and airborne key, plus two Walker poses (neutral and warning). These are separate key drawings, not rotated parts or a full walk/attack cycle. The GIF arranges them to expose the pose changes; it is not a video of physics gameplay. In Godot the same keys are selected by live movement, jump and warning state.
- [Eight-second sound direction](review/side_audio_direction.wav). Approximately 0–2 s: alternating steps; 2–4.2 s: two synthetic vocal takes; 4.4 s onward: two pistol takes, metal/concrete-style hits and a lower vocal reprise. The game uses preliminary gains against the existing original rain bed; reload uses the earlier custom cue. This is an audition for the mix, not a recording or a finished sound library.
- The exterior is the visual direction sample: one facade, baked roof/industrial layers and wet reflections, a shadowed 2D lamp and restrained moving rain. The interior is a neutral **navigation fixture**, not a second finished environment. Full parallax, fog/shader treatment and final 2D lighting are still open. The thin finite-trajectory marker is diagnostic; final flashes, casings, blood, decals, angled aim cels and staged melee art are not in the review build.

The visual samples use **AI-assisted pixel-art concept keys, then local mask, palette and anchor work**. They are not represented as fully hand-pixelled production animation. A few keys can demonstrate silhouette and material direction; they cannot establish consistency across a full cycle. If this direction is approved, the next step should be a manually corrected model sheet and a complete small motion test, not batch generation of the remaining frames. If either character still misses the intended premium look, narrow the pass to a manually redrawn single walk/aim or warning cycle before investing in the rest.

## Architecture and validation

`SideRoom` assembles the common floor, camera, physics actors and HUD. Each `SidePortal` points to a scene and a named arrival marker. A pending transfer snapshots health, magazine, reserve and the exterior Walker's current HP; it does not replicate transient projectiles, cooldowns or enemy positions. No depth sorting or y-lane collision feeds the active combat. The old lifted ground ray is retained only in the historical fixture. Here, a finite projectile sweeps its movement segment against world and enemy collision; a short shoulder-to-muzzle obstruction check prevents the barrel spawning a shot through cover. The original ammo capacity, reserve, reload duration, damage and attack warning timings remain recognizable. Pursuit, melee range and collision placement changed to suit the side-on bodies.

- **Passed on a source-built Linux headless Godot 4.7.2 runner**, with temporary runtime imports: horizontal movement, low-case obstruction, one jump and landing, airborne aim origin, finite travel, cover and damage, reload accounting, contextual two-way portal and state transfer, Walker warning/contact, melee finish and 2× viewport transform. Its fallback text server did not support RTL; this is not a UI shaping result.
- Reproduce with the standard editor binary: `godot --headless --path . --editor --import`, then `godot --headless --path . --script res://tests/side_gate_smoke.gd`. Windows editor/export, rendered scales, dynamic shadows, real-time mouse feel, audio on a physical device, and performance were **not tested** here.
- Regenerate review assets with `python tools/side_review/pack_art.py`, `python tools/side_review/pack_audio.py` and `python tools/side_review/make_review.py`. Artwork tooling needs Pillow 12+; the offline Persian layout fallback uses `arabic-reshaper` and `python-bidi` if Pillow lacks Raqm. Godot needs none of these Python packages. See [provenance and licensing](ASSET_LICENSES.md).
