# Phase 1 assets

Vazirmatn Regular is bundled unmodified for offline Persian text shaping. Source: [upstream font](https://github.com/rastikerdar/vazirmatn/blob/6e553e33489a8f9dfaccc76860a2e3f3c1e66de7/fonts/ttf/Vazirmatn-Regular.ttf). Redistribution is permitted under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the upstream copyright and full license accompany the font in [OFL.txt](../assets/fonts/OFL.txt). Include that notice when redistributing builds.

The placeholder silhouette, crate and lane are project-authored geometry; no external sprite or audio assets.

Phase 2: all four mono PCM cues in `assets/audio/` are original synthesized tones/noise, not recordings or third-party samples. The depot skyline, weapon and enemy silhouettes are original temporary procedural drawings. The HUD uses the same bundled OFL font.
